# PharmaLink Ethiopia — Product Roadmap

**Document Type:** Product Roadmap
**Product:** PharmaLink Ethiopia — Digital Healthcare Marketplace
**Version:** 1.0
**Prepared by:** Senior Business Analyst
**Status:** Draft for Review

## 1. Purpose
This roadmap sequences delivery of the platform across time-boxed phases, aligning features with business objectives, dependencies, risks, and KPIs. Timeframes are indicative and should be confirmed against team capacity, funding, and regulatory milestones.

## 2. Roadmap Themes
- **Foundation & Compliance:** Trust, identity, licensing, payments.
- **Core Marketplace:** Medicine discovery, prescriptions, delivery.
- **Healthcare Services:** Appointments, hospitals, diagnostics.
- **Scale & Intelligence:** Analytics, optimization, ecosystem expansion.

## 3. Phased Plan

### Phase 0 — Discovery & Compliance Foundation (Months 0–2)
- **Goals:** Validate scope, secure regulatory guidance, finalize architecture.
- **Deliverables:** Regulatory engagement plan, compliance requirements sign-off, technical architecture, integration agreements (Fayda ID, payment), design system.
- **Exit Criteria:** Approved MVP scope; confirmed launch geography; signed integration commitments.

### Phase 1 — Core Medicine Marketplace MVP (Months 2–6)
- **Scope (Must-have):** Registration/OTP, Fayda ID verification, medicine search & stock, prescription upload & pharmacist verification, pharmacy matching, cart/checkout, local payments & refunds, delivery dispatch & tracking, provider onboarding & license verification, notifications, admin & audit basics.
- **Target Users:** Customers and pharmacies in the initial launch city (e.g., Addis Ababa).
- **KPIs:** First 50+ verified pharmacies; median time-to-fulfillment < 3h; order success rate >= 90%; zero major safety incidents.
- **Exit Criteria:** Stable core loop, compliance controls verified, positive pilot feedback.

### Phase 2 — Diaspora & Trust Expansion (Months 6–9)
- **Scope (Should-have):** Cross-border ordering & payment, proof of delivery, ratings & reviews, provider dashboards & analytics, bulk inventory, multi-language completeness.
- **KPIs:** Diaspora orders live; CSAT >= 4.2; 150+ pharmacies; repeat-order rate growth.
- **Exit Criteria:** Reliable diaspora flow; provider self-service maturity.

### Phase 3 — Healthcare Services (Months 9–14)
- **Scope (Should-have):** Doctor appointment system, hospital/clinic directory, diagnostic/lab booking with preparation info, reminders.
- **KPIs:** 100+ providers with online scheduling; appointment utilization growth; reduced no-shows.
- **Exit Criteria:** Appointments and diagnostics adopted by target providers; scheduling reliability.

### Phase 4 — Enhancements & Optimization (Months 14–18)
- **Scope (Could-have):** Waiting lists, in-app messaging, lab result delivery (with integration), advanced search ranking, substitute recommendations, scheduled delivery, cash-on-delivery (if approved).
- **KPIs:** Engagement and retention uplift; delivery efficiency gains; support-ticket reduction.
- **Exit Criteria:** Measurable experience and efficiency improvements.

### Phase 5 — Scale & Ecosystem (Months 18+)
- **Scope:** Geographic expansion to more cities/regions; insurance integration; distributor/supplier integration; AI-driven recommendations and demand forecasting; deeper regulator reporting.
- **KPIs:** Multi-city coverage; positive contribution margin per order; 500+ pharmacies; growing GMV and active users.
- **Exit Criteria:** Sustainable unit economics; national scalability proven.
